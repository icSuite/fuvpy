# 2026-09-02 — Background uncertainty implementation and validation

## Outcome

Implemented the architecture through the experimental public interface while
preserving ordinary BS and SH results. Added conditional coefficient and
prediction covariance, corrected-image covariance with the shared-data cross
term, coherent sampling, repeated complete fits, dependent WIC BS-to-SH
propagation, compact frame-mean temporal covariance, diagnostics,
serialization metadata, documentation, and tests.

An independent code review confirmed the core algebra. Its findings led to
solver-consistency, convergence, provenance, units, default-cost, basis
reconstruction, and full-array regression-test fixes.

## Verification

- 18 automated tests passed before the final temporal-review corrections.
  Stricter temporal assertions were then added but not rerun at the user's
  request.
- Full-array legacy fingerprints protect BS and SH values and masks.
- Synthetic coefficient draws reproduce analytic prediction variance.
- NetCDF round trips retain the variance input and compact fit representation.
- Complete detector-grid orbits 214 and 1926 were used for WIC and SI13
  validation under residual-scale and count-scale perturbations.

## Durable findings

- Fixed-weight covariance reproduced repeated marginal background spread to
  roughly 10--15% in the tested bright count-scale cases and reproduced
  adjacent-frame correlation to within 0.005.
- Corrected-image one-sigma coverage was about 0.70 at the median because
  input noise dominates that field.
- Repeated robust fits can have a shifted mean relative to the unperturbed fit;
  this explains poor fitted-background coverage even when the spread ratio is
  near one. Covariance alone cannot encode this shift.
- WIC `dgsigma` is much broader than count scale and must not be labelled as a
  detector variance.
- The full WIC result is SH-dominated, while BS/SH covariance changes sign
  spatially. The dependent repeated chain is required; independent component
  variances are rejected.
- Ten to twenty refits are enough for marginal and short-lag diagnostics, not
  for a stable long-lag empirical covariance.

## Next boundary

The remaining blocker is scientific rather than architectural: define a
calibrated detector observation-variance model for WIC and SI. No downstream
icBuilder integration should present this prototype as total image
uncertainty before that is resolved.
